# LearnFlow Project Context

## Purpose

This is the mandatory entry point for LearnFlow. It gives a human or AI assistant enough orientation to locate authoritative decisions without relying on chat history.

LearnFlow is an AI-powered, extensible learning platform. GATE Computer Science is its first learning program, not a hard-coded product boundary. The platform is intended to support structured learning journeys such as examinations, courses, certifications, and interview preparation.

## Current project state

- Stage: documentation and architecture foundation, plus a minimal FastAPI backend foundation, the curriculum and examination-schedule persistence schema, the curated data that fills both, the first read API over the curriculum, and a Next.js frontend that reads it, through which a learner can complete setup — a profile, an active learning program, a study goal, the weekly study time they have available, and how they want a plan built — see that setup back on the home screen, record a learning stage against a topic while browsing the curriculum, generate an initial study plan from all of it, mark its work completed, skipped, or postponed, have the plan rebuilt around what they have and have not done, open a daily study view showing what that plan asks of them today, open a monthly study view showing where their calendar month sits in it, see whether the study time they saved covers what is left before their date, bring finished topics back for review, read where their study stands in one place — including what could use their attention now, and why — catalogue their own study material against the topics it covers and meet it again beside the plan items that name its topic, and write their own practice questions, take a checkpoint quiz built from them, read an honest per-question result, and revisit every quiz they have taken.
- Implemented: a FastAPI application built through a composition-root application factory; validated startup configuration for `APP_ENV`, `APP_LOG_LEVEL`, `APP_DEFAULT_TIMEZONE`, `API_HOST`, `API_PORT`, and the required `DATABASE_URL`; `GET /health`, an operational endpoint served outside `/api/v1`; the curriculum read endpoints CUR-001 to CUR-003 under `/api/v1/curriculum`; EXM-001, which reads the published examination schedules as reference data and resolves no learner; the learner-owned setup endpoints LRN-001, LRN-002, and GOAL-001 to GOAL-004, whose contracts are fixed by [ADR-016](adr/ADR-016-learner-onboarding-api-contracts.md), and GOAL-005, which replaces a goal's weekly availability a week at a time and is fixed by [ADR-018](adr/ADR-018-weekly-availability-slots.md); the planning preferences GOAL-001 and GOAL-004 accept and every goal response carries — a preferred session length and a topic order, each optional and neither defaulted — fixed by [ADR-019](adr/ADR-019-study-goal-planning-preferences.md); the first learner topic progress, PRG-004 recording a learning stage against a trackable topic and PRG-002 reading back what was recorded, contracted by [ADR-017](adr/ADR-017-topic-progress-api-and-schema.md) — all with the `data` envelope, `limit`/`offset` pagination, and the documented error envelope and [error codes](api/conventions.md#error-codes) applied to every failure; a Next.js + TypeScript frontend serving a home screen that reads back the learner's saved setup — the profile, their active study goal or, failing that, their most recent one, the goal's examination cycle with its window and every published examination period, and the study week and planning preferences saved against it — alongside a curriculum view that offers a learning-stage control beside each trackable topic and the learner setup screen that writes the goal, the week, and the preferences, configured by `API_BASE_URL` and calling the API from its own server — including its writes, which go through a server action — so no API address reaches a browser and, for as long as that holds, the backend needs no CORS middleware, per [ADR-015](adr/ADR-015-frontend-foundation-and-server-rendered-api-access.md); backend and frontend container images with Docker Compose `frontend`, `backend`, and `postgres` services, the frontend serving its own static `/health` for its container health check so a probe never calls the API; SQLAlchemy models plus Alembic migrations creating the curriculum tables, the examination schedule tables, `learners` and `study_goals`, `learner_topic_progress`, and `availability_slots`, and adding the two planning-preference columns to `study_goals`; an idempotent seed that loads the curated GATE CSE curriculum, described in [database migrations](database/migrations.md#the-curriculum-seed); a second idempotent seed that loads the published GATE 2027 examination schedule, described in [the examination schedule seed](database/migrations.md#the-examination-schedule-seed); and a command that binds the local learner to that curriculum and examination goal. Study plans complete the picture: PLN-001 generates a `roadmap` over every trackable topic and a `weekly` plan over the coming seven days, deterministically and with no AI provider, from the goal's horizon, the saved availability, the planning preferences, and any recorded stages, superseding rather than deleting whatever it replaces; PLN-002 and PLN-003 read them back, and a `/plan` screen shows them with the reason for every item — contracted by [ADR-020](adr/ADR-020-initial-study-plan-generation.md), with migration `20260806_03` creating `study_plans` and `plan_items`, and the first module in `backend/app/domain/` holding the two rules that decide a plan. PLN-004 then lets the learner act on that plan: it marks one item `completed` and puts it back to `planned`, writing the `status` and `completed_at` columns `20260806_03` created ahead of it — so it needed **no migration** — and the `/plan` screen offers the control beside every item on both panels. Completing is reversible, it moves that item alone, and nothing is counted or re-planned around it. Contracted by [ADR-021](adr/ADR-021-plan-item-completion.md). **PLN-005 then closes the loop**: the learner asks for their plan to be rebuilt around what happened, and adaptation leaves out every topic they have completed, marks work whose day passed `postponed` on the plan it supersedes, and re-places it on the new one — by the same deterministic rules generation uses, needing **no migration**. Nothing adapts on its own. Contracted by [ADR-022](adr/ADR-022-plan-adaptation.md). **A daily study view completes the picture for now**: `/plan/today` reads the goal's active `weekly` plan and shows the work it placed on the learner's own calendar date — resolved from `learners.timezone`, never the server's — with each item's reason and the same completion control, alongside work whose day has passed, which it shows and deliberately does not move. It is a *reading*, not a `daily` plan: that `plan_type` stays unwritten, and the view needed **no endpoint, no column, and no migration**. Contracted by [ADR-023](adr/ADR-023-daily-study-view.md). **Skipping then completed the plan-item statuses**: PLN-004 also accepts `skipped`, and a skip **settles the item, not the topic** — adaptation never overwrites it with `postponed`, and plans its topic again, unlike a completed one — which is what keeps a skip reversible once the plan it sits on is superseded. `plan_items.status` had no unwritten value left, and it needed **no migration, no column, and no endpoint**. Contracted by [ADR-024](adr/ADR-024-plan-item-skipping.md). **Postponement then made the last verb a learner action**: PLN-004 accepts `postponed` too, so a learner may move an item between all four statuses in any direction, and **FR-004's first acceptance criterion is met in full**. `postponed` now has two writers and one meaning — the learner saying *not yet*, and adaptation marking work whose day passed with nothing said about it — and postponing **moves nothing on its own**: it takes no date, re-dates nothing, and triggers no adaptation, so the work is placed again when the learner asks. A postponed item is **settled**, so adaptation leaves it exactly as marked and plans its topic again, and it is left out of *From earlier days* as a skipped one is. It needed **no migration, no column, and no endpoint** either. Contracted by [ADR-025](adr/ADR-025-learner-postponement.md). **A monthly study view then completed the plan levels a learner can read**: `/plan/month` shows where the learner's own calendar month — from `learners.timezone`, never the server's — sits in their plan, listing the days that month already has dated work on and then the roadmap topics their week has not dated, openly undated. Because a weekly plan dates seven days, a month is mostly undated, and the screen **says so rather than spreading the roadmap across the days that are left**: placing work on a day is planning, which the backend owns. It is **read-only** — no status control, no generate control, no adapt control — which is where it departs from the daily view, and it needed **no endpoint, no column, no migration, and no backend change at all**. That meets [FR-003](requirements/functional.md#fr-003-study-timeline-and-plan)'s second acceptance criterion in full. Contracted by [ADR-026](adr/ADR-026-monthly-study-view.md). **PLN-006 then closed the last gap in FR-004**: `GET /api/v1/study-goals/{study_goal_id}/plan-feasibility` reports whether the study time the learner saved covers the work left before their horizon, and `/plan` shows the verdict, the figures behind it, and — when time is short — how many topics that time does cover and what the learner could change. The arithmetic is a **pure domain rule**, the fourth in `backend/app/domain/study_planning.py`, because [terminology](domain/terminology.md) reserves totalling a week for the planner rather than a screen. **It is a live read and writes nothing** — no plan, no availability, no preference, no item status, and no adaptation — so the answer moves when the learner's week does, where a sentence stored in `generation_reason` would go stale. `unknown` is an answer, naming whether a date or a week is missing; a week saved and deliberately kept free is zero minutes rather than unknown. Everything is **counts and durations, never a ratio**. It needed **no migration**. Contracted by [ADR-027](adr/ADR-027-plan-feasibility.md). **Revision then delivered the built part of FR-006**, the last of Milestone 3's requirements to be started: REV-004 schedules revisions for topics the learner completed planned work on, REV-001 and REV-002 read them back, and REV-003 records what became of each — `completed`, `skipped`, `postponed`, or back to `due`, every move reversible, exactly as PLN-004 works for a plan item. **The learner asks; nothing schedules on its own**, and asking twice creates nothing the second time. A topic returns an interval after the work it follows, decided by the **learning stage the learner recorded** — LearnFlow's own intervals, named as its own — and a completed review schedules the next. A revision is **not a plan item**: it lives in `revision_records` and survives the supersede adaptation performs on every active plan, so `plan_items.action_type = 'revise'` stays unwritten and PLN-005 is untouched. **Nothing writes a learning stage.** This is the **first migration since `20260806_03`** — `20260813_01`, one table and one index, altering nothing. **FR-006 is not met in full**: seeing what is due and recording a completion or skip are, but the resource-and-practice half of its second criterion is **deferred** to FR-007 and FR-009. Contracted by [ADR-028](adr/ADR-028-revision-workflow.md). **A progress overview then gathered the result**: `/progress` shows where a learner's study stands — what each active plan covers, what today holds, whether the saved week reaches the date, what they have marked, and which topics are ready to review — built as a **reading** of six existing contracts, so it needed **no endpoint, no column, no migration, and no backend change at all**. **It writes nothing and counts nothing of its own**: the only figures on it are ones the API reported, because [terminology](domain/terminology.md) forbids counting skips, postponements, and reviews by name, so what a learner marked is listed rather than tallied. It is the screen the word *dashboard* was reserved for, and its canonical name is **progress overview**. **PRG-001 stays unimplemented.** Contracted by [ADR-029](adr/ADR-029-progress-overview.md). **That overview has since gained the learner's recorded learning stages, gathered by subject**: it joins PRG-002 to CUR-003 in the client — the join the curriculum view already performs, read the other way round — so it needed no endpoint, no column, no migration, and no backend change either, and `/progress` now reads eight contracts rather than six. Only topics the learner recorded something against appear, and a subject holding none is left out; the panel **lists and never counts** — no figure beside a subject name, and no ordering, grouping, or colouring by stage, because a learner may move to any stage from any stage. It **writes nothing**: recording a stage stays beside the topic in the curriculum view, which it links to. What is gathered is the *learning stage* alone, since `material_status` is not created and `study_activities` does not exist. Contracted by [ADR-030](adr/ADR-030-learning-stages-by-subject-panel.md). **It has since gained a priority focus panel at its head**, gathering three facts a backend rule already decided — work whose day has passed with nothing said about it, reviews REV-001 reports as due, and a saved week PLN-006 says does not reach the horizon — with the reason each entry is there, taken from the record itself. It needed **no endpoint, no read, no column, no migration, and no backend change either**. **It ranks nothing**: its groups sit in a fixed presentation order that orders nothing, no entry is numbered or capped, and the recorded *learning stage* is deliberately not a signal, because selecting some of the five stages would rank them against each other. It writes nothing and counts nothing. Contracted by [ADR-031](adr/ADR-031-priority-focus-panel.md). **PRG-001 now waits on the quiz, external-test, and mistake evidence alone.** **FR-011 is still not met in full** — two of its four acceptance criteria are met and a third is partly met. **A learning-resource catalogue then opened Milestone 4**: RES-001 to RES-004 record where a learner's own study material is and which topics it covers, `/resources` writes it, and the curriculum view and `/revisions` show a topic's material read-only — which supplies the **resource half** of FR-006's second criterion, deferred since [ADR-028](adr/ADR-028-revision-workflow.md). **A resource is metadata, never the material**: nothing is uploaded, extracted, or indexed, `storage_key`, `metadata`, and `resource_ingestions` are all absent, and **no location on the learner's own machine is stored** — a link is an `http` or `https` address, and anything offline is described in the learner's own words. **Nothing curated ships**, so there is no seed and no external content in the repository; **nothing is recommended, ranked, or counted**; and **nothing is deleted** — material is put aside reversibly, so RES-005 stays unimplemented. Migration `20260816_01` creates `resources` and `resource_topic_links`. **FR-007 is not met in full**: three of its four criteria are met, one of them partly, because a **path** to a local file is not stored. Proposed by [ADR-032](adr/ADR-032-learning-resource-catalogue.md). Checkpoint practice joins them: QZ-008 to QZ-010 hold the practice questions the learner writes, and QZ-001, QZ-002, QZ-003, QZ-005, QZ-006, and QZ-007 assemble a quiz from them, run an attempt, and read the result back — deterministically, with no AI provider, and with no score anywhere. **A checkpoint practice history then made every one of those attempts reachable**: `/practice/history` lists them a page at a time — newest first, with the topics each covered and what became of each question in words — and opens each attempt's existing result, while `/practice` keeps the most recent and links there. It is a **reading** of QZ-006, opening the QZ-007 result view ADR-033 already built, so it needed **no endpoint, no changed shape, no column, no migration, and no backend change at all**, and it uses the payload ADR-033 had recorded as a cost. **Nothing is counted, scored, or compared**: no score, mark, percentage, count of quizzes taken, streak, or comparison between attempts, and the pages are not numbered, because a page count is a count of the learner's quizzes with an extra step — `pagination.total`, which is exactly that count, is never read. **Paging is not capping**: the order is QZ-006's own and every attempt stays reachable. It is **read-only**, and `/progress` gains nothing, so FR-011's recent-quiz-history criterion stays unmet. Contracted by [ADR-034](adr/ADR-034-checkpoint-practice-history.md). **A learner may then correct a question until a quiz has asked it**, and sets it aside afterwards: a past result is assembled from the live question row, so an asked question's wording is fixed, while one nothing references has no history to rewrite. It rides QZ-010's existing `PATCH` and needs **no endpoint, column, table, or migration**; a question set aside is read-only until brought back, the content is replaced as one group, and a correction keeps the same record. **No past result changes.** Contracted by [ADR-035](adr/ADR-035-practice-question-correction.md). **A learner's catalogued material then reached the screens they plan from**: `/plan` and `/plan/today` show, beside each plan item, the material the learner linked to the topic it names — **read-only**, through the same `TopicResources` component and the same client-side topic join [ADR-032](adr/ADR-032-learning-resource-catalogue.md) wrote for the curriculum view and `/revisions`, so it needed **no endpoint, no changed shape, no column, no migration, and no backend change at all**. It **amends ADR-032 on one point of substance** — its sentence that the plan screens are untouched — and overturns none of its reasoning. **Nothing is recommended, ranked, or counted**: a topic's material is what the learner linked to it, in RES-002's order; a topic with nothing linked renders **nothing at all**; material neither reorders a plan nor explains one; and no figure appears beside an item, a day, a subject, or a plan. **No control is added** — registering, correcting, and putting material aside stay on `/resources`, which both screens name — and material put aside stays out. **`/plan/month` is deliberately unchanged**, because the month's value is its shape. An unreadable catalogue costs the material rather than the plan. **FR-007's four criteria are unchanged**, and FR-007 is still not met in full. Contracted by [ADR-036](adr/ADR-036-topic-material-on-the-plan-screens.md).

Adaptation still writes `postponed` as well, on the plan it supersedes, for items whose day passed with nothing said about them (PLN-005), so the status has two writers rather than one; the learner who asks for adaptation still does not choose what it postpones. **All three of FR-004's acceptance criteria are now met**, the third by PLN-006 above. What remains in Milestone 3 is a generated `monthly` or `daily` plan. **FR-003's second acceptance criterion is now met in full**: its daily recommendation by the `/plan/today` view and its monthly one by the `/plan/month` view, both of which *read* the generated roadmap and weekly plan rather than generating a plan of their own. Nothing still totals a week outside a plan, and no preference or topic is ranked or scored. Three of FR-005's six acceptance criteria are met — marking a topic with one of the five stages, updating it at any time, and showing an encouraging next action. The other three need material completion, study activity, and the remaining evidence kinds, none of which is stored: PRG-001, PRG-003, ACT-001, and ACT-002 therefore stay uncontracted, and `learner_topic_progress` is created without `material_status`, `material_completed_at`, or `last_studied_at`. Study-activity and external-evidence tables are migrated with the milestones that use them; the assessment tables arrived with migration `20260818_01`, per [ADR-011](adr/ADR-011-sqlalchemy-persistence-implementation.md); the resource area is **partly** migrated, without `storage_key`, `metadata`, or `resource_ingestions`, each of which waits on the code that would maintain it. Compose covers the frontend, backend, and PostgreSQL — the `chromadb` service joins it with the code that consumes it, per [Docker strategy](deployment/docker.md). Infer no application behavior beyond the implemented items above.

- Immediate objective: [Milestone 3](roadmap/milestones.md#milestone-3-planning-and-revision) is substantially complete. **FR-003 and FR-004 are met in full**: the monthly view closed FR-003's second criterion and PLN-006 closed FR-004's third. **FR-006 is not**: REV-001 to REV-004 with `revision_records` deliver its revision scheduling, status updates, and due-review view, but the **resource-and-practice half of its second criterion is deferred** to FR-007 and FR-009, which do not exist, and its fourth criterion considers three of its four inputs because no quiz or test evidence is stored. What is open in planning is a generated **`monthly`** or **`daily`** plan — both approved `plan_type` values whose contents are deliberately still undecided, and which the `/plan/month` and `/plan/today` views above are not. Earlier milestones keep the open items their own checklists record — the `chromadb` Compose service, and material status and study activity among them. **Milestone 2's progress-overview item is advanced but still open**: `/progress` exists and now gathers progress **by subject and topic** — the recorded learning stages, per [ADR-030](adr/ADR-030-learning-stages-by-subject-panel.md) — and **priority focus areas** now exist too, gathered from stored dates and statuses and ranking nothing — so what stays unbuilt there is a priority drawn from **external-test and mistake evidence**, which nothing stores. Quiz evidence *is* now stored, and deliberately feeds nothing: a checkpoint outcome moves no learning stage, plan, or revision, and no score exists to rank anything by. Each affected area still replaces relevant placeholders with approved project decisions before it is implemented. **[Milestone 4](roadmap/milestones.md#milestone-4-resources-rag-and-mentor) is now open**, on its first item alone: a learner catalogues their own study material, links it to topics, and meets it again on the curriculum, revision, and plan screens, and everything else there — extraction, indexing, ingestion status, retrieval, and the mentor — is unbuilt. **FR-007 is not met in full.** **[Milestone 5](roadmap/milestones.md#milestone-5-quiz-and-external-test-evidence) is now open too**, on its first two items: a learner writes their own practice questions and takes a checkpoint quiz assembled from them, deterministically and with no AI provider, and reads a result that states what became of each question and **no score at all** — the conflict between [terminology](domain/terminology.md) and [schema.md](database/schema.md), resolved in terminology's favour by [ADR-033](adr/ADR-033-checkpoint-practice-workflow.md). **No question content ships with LearnFlow.** The rest of that milestone — external test results, mistake evidence, and anything that incorporates quiz evidence into a recommendation — is unbuilt, and `mistake_evidence` cannot be created until the external-evidence tables two of its four discovery sources reference exist. **FR-009 is not met in full**, and neither is FR-006, whose practice half is deliberately left open because surfacing a quiz on a revision would mean recommending one.

## Non-negotiable engineering direction

- Keep business rules independent from infrastructure.
- Use focused modules with clear responsibilities.
- Put external systems behind interfaces or adapters where replacement is realistic: AI providers, storage, vector search, embeddings, and persistence.
- Treat documentation and ADRs as part of the deliverable.
- Prefer simple, predictable workflows over premature autonomous or multi-agent complexity.

## Workflow for AI assistants

1. Read this file and all documents named in the “Required reading by task” table.
2. Do not invent an architectural decision merely because a placeholder exists.
3. State conflicts, missing decisions, or broad scope changes before implementation.
4. Keep edits narrowly scoped to the assigned task.
5. Update linked documentation when implementation changes an approved behavior.
6. Do not overwrite a document marked `approved` without explicit direction and an ADR when needed.
